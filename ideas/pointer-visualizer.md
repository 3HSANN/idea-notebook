## Why this is interesting

Pointers click when you can see boxes and arrows move. A small stepper that draws the stack, the heap and each pointer would make them stick.

## Scope

- Paste a short C++ snippet from a fixed subset
- Step through it line by line
- Draw variables as boxes and pointers as arrows
